How the score works

Importance weights are divided by their sum. The score is 100 × the weighted sum of factor scores. Service access uses equal parts distance and count: ½ × (1 − min(distance/radius, 1)) + ½ × min(count/cap, 1). Daily transit averages bus and metro access. Airport terminals use 25 km. Workplace distance uses a 50 km cap. PM2.5 indicator score = 100 × (1 − min(value/250, 1)), using the nearest valid PM2.5 monitor within 5 km and 6 hours. Values must be nonnegative. The cap of 250 is a project normalisation choice, not a safe-air limit. Units and averaging period are not assumed; this indicator is not official AQI. These are project rules, not official standards.

Every positively weighted factor must have data. Missing factors never receive invented values or a different set of weights for each area. Extract bounds indicate extent, not guaranteed completeness. A zero means no suitable mapped evidence in range, not proof a service does not exist.
